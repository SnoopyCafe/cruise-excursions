Basseterre, St. Kitts

"Scenic Relaxation" — Turquoise water, coastlines & Caribbean cool

⭐ 1st Choice

Deluxe Catamaran Sail & Snorkel

Elegant catamaran sail with snorkeling, open bar, Caribbean music, a beach stop, and lunch. The classic Caribbean experience with a relaxed, mature-adult vibe and stunning coastline scenery.

~$240 for 2 ⭐ 4.7/5 Tripadvisor
2nd Choice

St. Kitts Scenic Railway + Island Tour

Historic narrow-gauge railway through rainforest, cliffs, villages, and sugar plantation scenery. Less physical activity, very scenic, and deeply cultural — a unique piece of Caribbean history.

~$300 for 2 ⭐ 4.6/5 Tripadvisor

🌊 Port Personality: "Scenic Relaxation"

Turquoise Water Scenic Coastline Snorkeling Open Bar Beach Time Caribbean Music Relaxed Pace

Philipsburg, St. Maarten

"Best Variety Island" — Dutch & French vibes, beaches & Maho magic

⭐ 1st Choice

Island Highlights + Maho Beach + French Side

Combines Dutch/French culture contrast, scenic overlooks, beach time, local rum tasting, and the famous low-flying jets at Maho Beach. Your "see everything" stop in one perfect day.

~$180 for 2 ⭐ 4.6–4.8/5
2nd Choice

Golden Eagle Catamaran to Tintamarre

Upscale sailing excursion with snorkeling, a gourmet lunch, open bar, and calmer waters near Tintamarre Island. An excellent luxury beach day if you prefer a more serene escape.

~$260 for 2 ⭐ 4.8/5 Tripadvisor

✈️ Port Personality: "Best Variety Island"

French Cafés Beach Bars Mountain Overlooks Airplane Landings Rum Tasting Dutch-French Culture Caribbean Energy

San Juan, Puerto Rico

"Best Culinary Stop" — Mofongo, rum & colorful colonial history

⭐ 1st Choice

Old San Juan Food & Walking Tour

Outstanding culinary and history experience through Old San Juan with mofongo, rum cocktails, local desserts, and historic architecture. Excellent for mature travelers wanting culture + flavors without heavy physical activity.

~$330 for 2 ⭐ 4.9/5
2nd Choice

El Yunque Rainforest & Luquillo Beach

Tropical rainforest with waterfalls and a beach stop with local kiosks and authentic Puerto Rican street food. More active, but breathtakingly beautiful. Note: ship departs early afternoon — plan accordingly.

~$220 for 2 ⭐ 4.7/5

🍽️ Port Personality: "Best Culinary Stop"

Authentic Mofongo Rum Tastings Colonial Architecture Colorful Streets Puerto Rican Culture Walkable Local Guides

Perfect Day at CocoCay

"Recovery & Luxury" — Infinity pool, premium dining & ocean views

⭐ 1st Choice

Coco Beach Club Day Pass

Upscale beach club with a quieter atmosphere, infinity pool, elevated dining, and premium beach loungers. A calmer, more refined vibe than the main island — perfect for the final day before heading home.

~$220 for 2 ⭐ Highly Rated
2nd Choice

South Beach Floating Cabana

Private overwater-style floating cabana with personal attendant service and panoramic ocean views. A splurge-worthy, unforgettable relaxation experience — one of CocoCay's most luxurious offerings.

~$500–700 for 2 ⭐ Top Luxury Pick
